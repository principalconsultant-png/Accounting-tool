# Business Pulse — Monthly Performance acceptance

Use a disposable demo book. Financial reports → Business Pulse opens a separate large window. Select April–March financial year, through date, unit and money display. Future/no-data cells are N/A; unmapped payroll/compliance cells remain N/A. Existing books are not changed by opening/exporting the report.

1. Post one earned receipt split across cash/UPI/card. Patient visits is one, collections reflect each split and ARPP is earned revenue divided by one. Settle UPI into bank: this does not create a second patient or increase collections.
2. Enter supplied new-patient counts through Monthly counts / review or the blank Excel/CSV input template. Blank means unavailable; zero means confirmed zero. Review duplicate month/unit and invalid counts in the upload preview. Saving a selected valid row adds an audited operational observation, with no accounting journal. Actual visit overrides are optional. Check ALL and unit inputs separately; partial-month through dates must not use full-month counts.
3. Select dedicated revenue, salary, overtime and compliance ledgers in Review ledger mappings and enter the review basis. Salary/OT must be separate; bank/AP/tax control mappings are rejected. Salary is cash paid, compliance is expense. Unidentifiable mixed withholding allocations require review instead of a guessed split.
4. Record petty expenses, stock purchases, staff advances and salary settlements. All are included in petty disbursements; Cash top-up is excluded. The rows may overlap other payments and must not be added as a total expense. Reverse a petty entry in a later month and inspect that month's negative adjustment.
5. Part-pay one AP invoice twice in one month. Total vendor payments reflects both amounts; invoice count is one. Later reversals affect their posting month.
6. Four or more observations with variation receive navy/blue/peach/coral colours from each row's own history through that month. Earlier colours must not change when a future observation is added. High costs are not labelled good. Click cells for source references and basis.
7. Export PDF/Excel. Check unit, year, through date, missing data, coverage and legibility. Configure client/AXIOM logos in Financial reports → Report logos and verify left/right positioning. Excel source details use actual INR, even when display uses thousands/lakh; ARPP remains INR.

Automated tests cover receipt splits/settlements, read-only snapshots, missing/zero observations, scope, month-to-date, imports/atomic rollback, mappings, paid allocation ambiguity, petty treatments/reversals, AP partial counts/reversals, historical colours, typed exports and the standalone desktop. Packaged self-test exercises calculations and both exports/templates. The downloadable sample uses synthetic training figures only.
