# B17 Income Tax WDV working-paper acceptance

Development 0.19.0-dev uses vault format 14. Test disposable books first and keep the verified original external-drive backup before upgrading formats 1–13. B16 book SLM remains separate. This module does not post book journals, compute taxable-profit absorption/unabsorbed-depreciation balances, file returns or certify tax compliance.

1. Open **Tax depreciation** and choose FY. The legal entity owns each tax block; JP Nagar, Kammanahalli and Vaikom are management-attribution units. Even branch-selected exports retain the entity tax summary with an explicit scope caption.
2. **Create tax block** with a unique category/rate key and opening FY. Pool the same statutory class/rate across all three units; do not create separate statutory blocks per branch. Equal rates alone do not make distinct statutory asset classes the same block. Enter `unit,wdv,count` opening allocations and the source basis. Counts cover existing in-use assets/components; positive opening WDV needs a positive control count. Unused capital is separate. Revise opening allocations only while dependent tax years are open. Unused incorrect block setups can be deactivated with a reason after withdrawing dependent facts.
3. **Save tax rate policy** with effective FY, WDV rate, short-use threshold/factor, rounding and the actual governing year/AY/regime source. Reference categories and legacy Act/Appendix references are proposals, not current-law approval. Use the applicable Act/rules for the selected year. Additional depreciation starts disabled/zero; enable only after reviewing the business/regime. Individual assets still need eligibility review.
4. **Register tax addition** for one asset/component with unique reference, original tax attribution unit, acquisition date and actual tax cost. Choose a book asset and **Use selected book asset facts** for proposals, or use a manual source. Explain actual-cost differences in the reviewed basis. A book source is snapshotted; its live acquisition cannot be counted twice. There is no acquisition journal in this action.
5. Leave first use blank for uncommissioned capital. **Record first use** when supported by actual commissioning evidence. Previously used assets before the opening FY belong in opening WDV. A later first-use event does not rewrite a frozen earlier pending-cost report.
6. Test inclusively counted 180 versus 179 actual use days. Current-FY acquisitions used less than the configured threshold get the short-use factor. Count only through the earlier of disposal and 31 March. Assets acquired in a prior FY and first used in the current FY follow the acquisition-year proviso treatment; verify that treatment against the governing year-specific law.
7. **Record tax disposal** with unique reference, actual tax attribution unit, date, gross moneys payable including applicable insurance/scrap, and eligible transfer expenses separately. Select the exact addition where applicable (count one); otherwise allocate only opening control counts. Source-cohort reductions occur before excess offsets the other cohort. Do not treat one branch's loss as a separate statutory block loss.
8. Test continuing versus ceased blocks. Continuing excess proceeds can produce a block capital gain; a ceased block gets no normal depreciation and its net capital gain/loss is shown. Expenses affect capital results, not gross WDV reductions. Pre-use sales stay outside the depreciable pool and require separate capital-result review. Final tax-return treatment is reviewed by the bookkeeper/tax adviser.
9. When unused capital remains after in-use assets cease, the working stays draft pending **Review block status**. Explicitly choose the supported statutory continuity/allowance treatment and basis. Continuing without an allowance preserves eligible basis without inventing counts; contradictory counts remain draft. Review current-year additional claims separately. Opening source/control counts and actual pending assets must support the chosen status.
10. Additional depreciation is never inferred from being a hospital asset. For a specifically eligible new asset, document eligibility and business/regime permission. Inspect first-year claim/deferred maxima. Short-use deferred portions carry once at the original frozen rate/entitlement. Disposal/cessation, regime disallowance, unsupported allowance and basis caps need **Review additional claim**; explicit zero/zero records reviewed forfeiture. Claims cannot bypass a disallowed/ceased block or exceed entitlements. The working does not calculate profit-absorption tax losses.
11. Check **Tax unit allocation bridge**: opening plus full/short additions minus gross realizations plus pooling/capital adjustments minus normal/additional allowances equals closing allocation. Unit totals must equal the entity. Positive net-basis weights, remaining-count/pending-cost fallbacks and largest-remainder rounding are documented management allocations. Source attribution is retained; a physical B16 transfer within the same legal entity is not a new tax acquisition/disposal.
12. **Finalize tax year** only after FY-end, resolving draft issues and finalizing prior years. It freezes the complete entity report and carries closing WDV/counts/deferred entitlements to the next year. It is a reviewed working snapshot, not tax-return filing. Future/in-progress years remain provisional. Reopen the newest finalized year first with a reason before correcting prior facts. Original snapshots remain in history.
13. Correct source mistakes using **Void selected tax source** with a reason; dependent claims/disposals must be withdrawn before an addition. Never delete history or duplicate cost. Linked book reversals dated inside finalized tax years are blocked until tax review is reopened. Later book reversals flag future tax-source review rather than rewriting frozen earlier reports.
14. Inspect each frozen preview before approval. Editing the books makes the preview stale. All tax actions, links, finalization, reopening and exports must leave the posted book journal count unchanged.
15. Export **Excel**, **PDF** and JSON. Verify entity WDV blocks; unit bridge; actual addition/use days; unused/excluded assets; additional review; rate/regime policy; and opening/transaction sources. Confirm numeric Excel cells, printed PDF tables (including policy/source bases), draft/provisional/finalized captions and correct selected scope. Reload the vault and repeat the carry-forward and original-backup checks on Windows.

Synthetic controls include 30 backend cases, two desktop flows and packaged self-test coverage. Example: opening WDV INR 1,000 plus a current-year INR 500 short-use addition at 15%/half rate gives INR 187.50 normal depreciation and INR 1,312.50 closing WDV, before any disposals/additional allowance. All reference rates, cost qualifications, actual use, capital-result and additional-eligibility decisions need client/year-specific acceptance.
