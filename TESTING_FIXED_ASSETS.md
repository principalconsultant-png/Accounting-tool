# B16 Companies Act book fixed assets

Use disposable books first. Development 0.18.0-dev uses vault format 13; preserve the original external-drive backup before upgrading formats 1–12. The application implements the BRD's monthly straight-line book method. Income Tax block depreciation is separate (B17).

1. Open **Assets & depreciation** and select the financial year, unit/entity and report dates. The initial as-of date is capped at today; refresh after editing dates.
2. **Register book asset**: give a unique reference/component name, unit, acquisition/put-to-use/book-baseline dates, capitalized cost, PPE/accumulated/expense ledgers and a reviewed Schedule II classification/source basis. Common reference lives are proposals, not automatic legal approval. For a specific medical-equipment category enter the reviewed life. **Use reference life proposal** never checks the review box for you.
3. Choose **Link existing** for acquisition or opening journals already posted. Enter cost and, when applicable, accumulated-depreciation source voucher IDs. Capacity is allocated per ledger/unit and cannot be used twice. Sources must be live original journals on the baseline date. An opening asset needs assessed remaining life and an opening/override basis. Land uses zero months and residual equal to cost. Existing legacy calculator rows remain visible; explicitly map their zero-based index after source review.
4. Choose **Post purchase** only for a new acquisition not posted elsewhere. This posts cost to PPE against the selected balance-sheet counter ledger, with zero opening depreciation and acquisition-date baseline. GST/TDS/fees are handled separately or in the existing acquisition journal. Separate additions/components receive separate references/source allocations. Never duplicate a previously posted cost.
5. Review original source and proposed journals in the preview, then **Approve asset action**. Changing fields after preview cannot alter its frozen result. Changing the books makes that preview stale and approval must fail until refreshed.
6. **Post book depreciation** monthly or as a catch-up. Review coverage and posting dates. For INR 1,200 cost, zero residual and 12 months from 1 April, April's charge is INR 100. From 16 April, April's charge is INR 50 and May's INR 100. Prior depreciation is deducted; a repeated run posts nothing. Cumulative rounding prevents drift and final charges stop at residual. Earlier years and existing period/year/bank locks apply.
7. If book depreciation already exists in manual journals, select the asset and **Link prior depreciation**. Allocate only unused source expense-debit/accumulated-credit capacity. Linking creates no journal. A historical link may expose prior-period discrepancies; dated reversals and current-period catch-up preserve those historical facts rather than rewriting old journals. Register all PPE/accumulated sources so the reconciliation agrees before posting further movements; unexplained manual movements block duplicate depreciation.
8. For eligible extra-shift use, select **Record extra-shift usage** and supply actual dates, double (1.5) or triple (2) multiplier and source basis. Explicitly confirm the asset is not NESD. Overlapping dates and changes to already-covered periods are blocked. Withdraw an unused shift record with a reason; reverse dependent postings/withdraw dependent estimates before changing covered records. Actual statutory eligibility must be reviewed.
9. **Revise estimate** prospectively after posting depreciation through the preceding day. Enter assessed remaining months/residual and technical basis. Past targets remain unchanged. Residual cannot exceed carrying amount. Withdraw only the latest unused revision; dependent journals must be reversed first.
10. **Transfer asset** to another unit. Preview prior-day depreciation in the old unit and transfer gross cost/accumulated depreciation with balanced inter-branch clearing. From the transfer date, charges belong to the new owner. Check a midmonth transfer and compare unit schedules/ledger balances to the entity.
11. **Dispose asset** using reviewed proceeds excluding separately accounted taxes. Preview outstanding depreciation through the disposal day, cost/accumulated derecognition and gain/loss. Blank gain/loss codes create dedicated free ledgers without reusing occupied codes. Review GST/TDS/fees independently and avoid proceeds already posted in another journal. Test sale at profit/loss and zero-proceeds scrapping.
12. Select an asset event and **Reverse selected asset journal**. Later active asset events and estimate dependencies must be reversed/withdrawn first. Exact dated reversals retain previous as-of balances; repeat reversal follows the chain. Acquisition/opening sources can be reversed from Vouchers only after dependent asset movements/estimates are cleared. Source reversals deactivate that source-linked asset as of the reversal date, preserving its historical record.
13. Export **Excel**, **PDF** and JSON. Check register original cost/lifetime depreciation/closing NBV, monthly expected and journal-date posted charges, PPE/accumulated-ledger reconciliation, estimate/source basis and shift history. Disposed assets retain historical cost/depreciation with zero closing NBV. Catch-up and dated reversals can make monthly expected-versus-posted timing differences; the closing target and ledger proof are separate controls. PDF is printable.
14. Reload and confirm source/policy/event persistence, unchanged journal count for links/exports and original-byte migration backup. Test the verified Windows executable with actual Excel/PDF printing and one reviewed client asset example per category before acceptance.

The software's reviewed/reconciled status records explicit configuration and accounting proofs. It does not certify statutory applicability or replace source/technical review. Useful-life reference proposals, NESD/shift eligibility and residual overrides require client/year-specific confirmation. Revaluation/impairment and statutory disclosures are not supplied by this monthly-SLM workflow.
