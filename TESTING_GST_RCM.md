# Domestic GST reverse charge — unpublished 0.13.0-dev

This draft continues B08 with **GST reverse charge** in the desktop navigation. It preserves prior GST/2B/notes, tax masters, history, bank, FX and ledger work. No new executable, GitHub push or environment publication is included. The public Windows executable remains 0.5.0. Windows/client acceptance and actual legal applicability remain pending.

## Upgrade and controls

Format/schema 9 adds RCM records and control-ledger mappings. Formats 1–8 remain readable. The first upgraded save retains the existing external original-file backup/byte verification safeguard and checks saved-state integrity before migration. Keep the original backup; older executables cannot open format 9.

RCM control ledgers are allocated only when needed, using free asset codes 1180–1199 and liability codes 2230–2299. Existing client codes are never reused or overwritten. Preview shows proposed controls; successful approval creates them atomically with the entry. Pending ITC review, CGST/SGST/IGST electronic cash and CGST/SGST/IGST RCM payables are separate protected controls. A failed/cancelled preview/post does not add masters or records.

## The four stages

1. **RCM purchase:** enter branch/recipient GSTIN, invoice date/reference, supplier GSTIN when available or a name when unregistered, supplier state, reviewed place of supply, base amount excluding RCM, cost/PPE/inventory/CWIP ledger and supplier payable/payment ledger. Select a reviewed effective-dated taxable GST rule and record the independently verified RCM applicability basis. The app does not decide that RCM applies from a vendor name or expense type.
2. Choose whether ITC is expected after payment and later review. Expected tax is debited to a pending-review asset, not input GST; otherwise it is included in cost. The supplier is credited only for the purchase base, while RCM tax is credited to separate RCM payable controls. Supplier-state/place-of-supply checks determine the split; unregistered suppliers still require reviewed state details. A registered supplier invoice already entered as forward-charge GST cannot also be entered as RCM, or vice versa.
3. **Electronic-cash deposit:** record the actual bank/challan funding date/reference and actual CGST/SGST/IGST cash allocations. This debits the electronic-cash controls and credits the selected INR bank. A challan deposit does not discharge the tax liability and does not allow an ITC entry by itself. Multiple deposits can fund one purchase's tax; components/branches/registrations cannot be silently interchanged. Existing portal cash/openings or other manual movements need separate reconciliation rather than an invented new bank deposit.
4. **Record tax discharge:** select the purchase, actual cash-utilisation date/reference and supporting narration. This debits RCM payable and credits electronic cash. Record actual use of cash against liability, not just bank funding. Full linked tax must be covered by tagged deposits and the book cash controls. The app does not file a return or verify a portal utilisation online. Amounts and component allocations must agree to the real utilisation record.
5. **Review ITC claim:** only after an active linked cash discharge, select the purchase/date/reference and explicitly confirm documents, payment, eligibility and timing. This debits the normal input GST ledgers and credits pending review. The entry is rejected if ITC was not expected, no cash discharge exists, pending balance is insufficient or another active claim exists. It is bookkeeping based on the bookkeeper's review, not independent legal certification or return filing.

Every stage requires a prepared journal preview followed by explicit approval. Changing fields or the books invalidates the preview. Existing inactive-branch/account, period/year and bank reconciliation locks continue to apply.

## Review, reversal and testing

- Review the dated stage register, RCM control-ledger opening/movement/closing amounts and unclassified manual differences. Signs are debit-positive. Outstanding rows show unpaid RCM tax and amounts still pending ITC review, including earlier years.
- GST accounting input totals include separately recorded RCM Claim movements and their dated reversals. Its invoice-base totals remain forward-charge only; RCM purchase bases are on the RCM page. RCM payable and cash funding must not be counted as ordinary output tax or claimed input credit.
- Reverse claim before discharge, and discharge before purchase or its funding deposit. Dependence checks include future/backdated allocations; electronic cash cannot be spent twice or taken from another registration/branch. Dated exact reversals preserve history and signed reports. Corrected purchase/deposit references must remain explicit and unique.
- Export the RCM review JSON, including vouchers, original rate/basis, source references, control mapping, proofs and outstanding amounts. Exporting does not post. Restart and check all stages and controls persist. Bank reconciliation can match actual deposit bank lines through its existing reviewed workflow.
- Test cancellation/stale previews, insufficient cash/components, duplicate invoices/deposits/actions, ITC before discharge, unreviewed eligibility and reversal ordering using invented books only. The default example test rate is synthetic; it does not establish any current statutory rate or RCM applicability.

## Current limits

Discharge and ITC are full-document actions; guided partial discharge/partial ITC, mixed eligibility and later eligibility reclassification remain pending. Domestic forward-charge 2B matching does not reconcile RCM sections; explicit RCM flags in that normalized input are rejected rather than ignored. Portal adapters, export/SEZ, mixed-rate entry, RCM note/amendment treatment, special interest/penalty entries, cash-ledger transfers/refunds, broader GST utilisation and filing are unfinished. Use separately reviewed manual entries where necessary and reconcile the resulting unclassified movements. B08 remains PARTIAL.

Automated tests cover complete stages, separation of bank funding/discharge, eligible/ineligible cost treatment, component/branch/registration isolation, multiple deposits, duplicate/date/future allocation and reversal guards, state/supplier validation, control-code collisions/protection, preview atomicity, forward/RCM duplication, outstanding carry-forward, explicit 2B RCM rejection, format-8 external-backup migration, exports and desktop preview/post/reload. Windows DPAPI and the extended packaged self-test require a future authorized Windows build.

Change history's voucher trace also includes the linked RCM purchase/discharge/claim records. Claim snapshots retain explicit purchase and actual-discharge voucher references. A bank deposit belongs to the component cash pool; the report does not invent a one-to-one allocation from one challan to one invoice. For purchases already recorded in generic manual journals, review the existing entry before using this full-purchase workflow to avoid recording the base cost twice; guided tax-only attachment/reclassification remains pending.
