# B08 domestic GST accounting — unpublished 0.10.0-dev

The new **GST accounting** page adds reviewed domestic forward-charge invoices, working GST summaries and offline matching against normalized GSTR-2B invoice data. Prior tax configuration/history/bank/FX/ledger work is preserved. No new executable or environment publication is included; the public Windows download remains 0.5.0. Windows and client acceptance remain pending.

## Upgrade safely

GST documents and 2B reconciliation records introduce format/schema 6. Formats 1–5 load in memory; the first upgraded save still requires an external-drive folder and a byte-verified copy of the original vault before replacement. Existing saved-state checkpoints are checked before migration. Preserve the original backup because older executables cannot open format 6.

## Domestic GST posting

1. In **Tax setup**, create/review a GST rule with the actual effective date, rate, treatment, applicability, source and optional linked revenue/cost ledgers. Use invented values in test books. The application does not supply independently verified current law.
2. Open **GST accounting → New GST invoice**. Select Sale or Purchase, branch, invoice date/reference, the actual GST registration and reviewed Indian place-of-supply state code. Registration numbers can remain blank in general setup, but this GST posting workflow requires the registration used on the invoice. Enter separate branch GSTIN/state details in Setup & periods before using a registration different from the entity GSTIN.
3. Select the reviewed rule and enter the amount as exclusive or inclusive of GST. Enter the revenue/cost ledger and the INR payment/receivable/payable control ledger. Purchase cost can be an expense, inventory or PPE ledger; bank/payment/GST ledgers cannot be selected as the purchase cost. Party-specific rules and self-invoices need separate review.
4. For a Sale, set ITC to Not applicable. B2C customer GSTIN may remain blank. For a taxable Purchase, enter the supplier GSTIN and explicitly choose Eligible or Ineligible ITC. Eligibility is the bookkeeper's assessment, not a result inferred by the app. Ineligible GST is added to cost; eligible GST goes to input-tax ledgers. An intra-state invoice charged outside the recipient registration's state requires separate eligibility review.
5. Prepare the journal preview. Check the supplier registration state, place of supply, taxable/base amount, CGST/SGST or IGST and every ledger line against the actual invoice. Purchases use the supplier's state to determine the tax split, not merely the recipient's state. Calculations use integer paise/Decimal with half-up rounding; any odd split paise is allocated to SGST. If the actual invoice needs another rounding adjustment or more complex lines, use a reviewed manual compound journal and reconcile its unclassified tax movement separately.
6. Approve only after review. Changing a field invalidates the prior preview. Posting is atomic and respects inactive accounts/branches, branch balancing, financial-year/period locks and bank reconciliation locks. Duplicate normalized invoice references for the same registration/counterparty/year are blocked. Later rule corrections cannot change stored document amounts or rate/source snapshots.
7. Correct posted invoices through the existing dated reversal workflow. Reversal movements appear in their actual posting period and retain the original document snapshot. Reversing a reversal is also reflected. A replacement requires an explicit new correction reference in this build; full tax credit/debit-note workflows remain pending.

## Summaries and handover

The global financial-year and branch selectors filter the GST working summary and document register. It shows sales/purchase base, input/output tax and tax added to cost. Turnover remains separated by GST registration and treatment in the exported working report. CSV and JSON exports include document/rule references and tax-ledger opening, movement, closing and unclassified/manual amounts. Exports are read-only.

Input-tax balances use debit-positive signs and output-tax balances credit-positive signs. Initial openings are shown separately from period movements. Manual journals, bank-fee GST and other tax movements outside this document workflow appear as unclassified differences; the summary does not silently turn them into classified GST invoices. Net ledger movement is not a GST return or payment-due calculation. Review each registration separately; there is no automatic cross-registration offset, ITC utilisation or filing.

## Offline 2B review

1. Download the **2B CSV template**. Prepare one aggregate row per positive domestic supplier invoice with `supplier_gstin,invoice,date,taxable,cgst,sgst,igst,itc_available`. Amounts are INR, dates YYYY-MM-DD and availability Y/N. A single-sheet XLSX with identical headers is also accepted. This is a normalized import, not a raw government portal JSON/XLSX extractor. Keep the original portal statement separately and verify normalization.
2. Choose **Import normalized 2B**, provide the recipient GSTIN and statement period YYYY-MM, review the invoice count and approve import. No journal is created. Invalid rows, formula amount cells, conflicting tax splits, duplicate invoice/date records and repeated files are rejected atomically. Credit notes, RCM and import-of-goods/services sections are outside this invoice-only template.
3. Select the import and choose **Review 2B import**. Review supplier GSTIN, invoice reference, date, taxable base and each tax component. Reference punctuation is normalized only to propose candidates; every link requires explicit review and exact date/base/tax amounts. Mismatches cannot be approved as exact. A book invoice cannot be reused across rows/import periods.
4. Inspect statement invoices missing from books and book purchases absent from the imported statement. Missing-2B checks cover book invoices dated in that statement period; older/late-reported invoices need separate review. An already-linked invoice is labelled rather than guessed missing.
5. Linking never changes source availability or book ITC treatment. Availability N remains visible even if the invoice is linked; a link is not an ITC claim. Reverse a linked purchase and confirm the earlier link is flagged for review. Undo links with a reason. An unused mistaken import can be voided after undoing active links; history remains, and corrected data can be reimported.
6. Export the 2B review JSON, including missing rows, source filename/digest, availability and candidate/link status. Restart and verify imports/links/documents persist. Configuring, importing or matching 2B must not increase posted journal counts.

## Remaining GST scope

Export/SEZ zero-rated documents, reverse charge, tax credit/debit notes, mixed-rate invoice lines, raw portal-format adapters, GST payments/utilisation and filing workflows require further build. Use reviewed manual journals where appropriate; their tax movement remains explicitly unclassified until supported document treatment is available. Hospital exemptions, place of supply and ITC eligibility always require client/year-specific review. This module does not certify a return or tax position. B08 remains PARTIAL in the tracker for this reason.

Automated tests cover intra/inter-state sales/purchases, inclusive/exclusive and odd-paise rounding, eligible/ineligible ITC, exempt treatment, branch/registration controls, duplicates/locks, rate snapshots, dated reversals/chains, manual/opening proofs, CSV/XLSX 2B import, mismatch/availability/reuse controls, two-way missing items, cancellation history, read-only safe exports, format-5 external-backup migration and desktop preview/approval/2B/reload. Windows encryption and packaged readiness checks still require a future authorized Windows build.
