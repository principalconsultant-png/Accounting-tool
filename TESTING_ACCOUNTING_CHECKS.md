# B23 checks and offline questions

Use disposable books → Checks & questions. Without reviewed policy, monetary and ageing thresholds are disabled; duplicate, negative-cash, CWIP and registered-source TDS checks still work. Review thresholds: blank disables, zero is explicit, nonnegative INR/day values only, effective date and basis required. These are bookkeeping prompts, not statutory determinations.

Test daily payee cash totals including split payments across units in entity scope; professional-fee/rent FY candidate totals; B09 actual source/PAN cumulative shortfall including late sources; party/reference duplicates across months of the same FY; exact journal candidates; CWIP/retention source differences, category budget overruns, missed milestones, negative cash and aged UPI/card receipts. Unknown payees and unregistered expense bases require manual review. Candidate expenses are not automatically a legal TDS base. Historical clearing must remain outstanding before a future settlement/reversal; later activity must not erase an earlier report.

Select a finding, record Investigating/Explained/Action required and evidence. Review is retained but does not suppress or correct the finding. Changed source amounts, policy or reversals generate fresh findings as applicable; stale snapshots cannot be reviewed. All posting corrections use existing approved workflows.

Questions use explicit From/Through and unit selectors. Try cash balance, bank balance, 1010 balance, rent expense, total expenses, revenue, profit, compare profit, top expenses and CWIP. Generic bank balance covers configured Cash and bank ledgers excluding main Cash 1000; use an account code for a specific bank or another cash ledger. Balance answers include all history through end; period income/expense/profit exclude opening/closing entries. Source trace identifies voucher, line number, unit, account, debit/credit and signed answer effect. Comparatives include both periods' source traces. Date/branch conflicts, unsupported purposes, payments/receipts, actions, causal/predictive/legal requests must ask for clarification rather than substitute another answer. Source reports remain available for allocation-specific questions.

Answers distinguish posted facts from review suggestions; no network, generative model or posting occurs. Export findings and answer packs to Excel/PDF, inspect full evidence/source IDs, numeric INR cells, literal formula-like text, period/scope and paginated headings. Edited selectors recalculate before export. Save/reload thresholds/reviews; customer-language and actual Windows-PC acceptance remain separate from CI.
