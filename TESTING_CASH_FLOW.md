# B21 cash-flow acceptance

Use disposable books first. Version 0.16.0-dev uses vault format 11. Choose an external backup folder before upgrading; keep the original-byte backup for the older executable.

1. Open **Cash flow setup**, choose the financial year and operating unit/entity. Review cash/bank/equivalent ledger codes, the complete classification map, noncash P&L adjustment codes, effective date and written policy basis. Save a reviewed policy. Defaults are proposals requiring review.
2. Post a service receipt, rent payment, patient bill/partial collection, supplier/TDS payment, cash equipment purchase, loan and depreciation. Direct operating/investing/financing totals and indirect profit/working-capital/noncash bridge must agree. Opening plus movements must equal closing cash. Initial opening is counted once; midperiod openings remain a review issue.
3. Buy equipment on a generic creditor, then pay it. The settlement must require review. Record its actual investing cash allocation with source basis. Test a mixed cash/noncash loan-funded purchase and an asset disposal/loss; do not claim gross proceeds unsupported by bank/source evidence. The gross-source checkbox is an explicit independent confirmation.
4. Transfer cash internally and between units. Internal entity transfers are excluded. Branch funding remains visible; the source pack explains branch/entity classification differences. Compare opening and closing cash across all three units to the entity.
5. Test foreign-currency cash held and converted to INR: carrying value, exchange effect and separate fees must reconcile. Reverse the conversion and confirm opposite effects. INR-converted receipts remain normal INR cash with source FX evidence.
6. Review all Draft reasons. Zero proof alone does not clear missing policy, unmapped accounts, mixed settlements or composite transactions. Record justified signed voucher allocations; exact reversals inherit opposite signs. New policy revisions are effective dated and existing revisions remain in history. End-date policy applies consistently across the selected reporting period.
7. Export the cash-flow Excel/PDF support pack and JSON evidence. Check both methods, voucher detail, noncash support, policy basis and entity branch reconciliation. In **Financial reports**, choose either cash-flow mode and custom dates; the combined financial pack now has nine report tabs plus Contents. Draft/reviewed status must appear in exports. Printing uses PDF.
8. Reload the vault and confirm policy/allocation persistence and no extra journals. Historical policies protect referenced ledgers from deletion. Existing formats migrate with original external backup. Repeat on the Windows executable and review local Excel/PDF rendering before client acceptance.

Automated synthetic coverage: 24 backend cases plus desktop workflow, full regression suite and packaged self-test. Reporting does not post, infer unsupported gross cash, certify statutory compliance or eliminate the need to review actual Ind AS 7 policy/presentation and source completeness. A reviewed software status records explicit classification review and arithmetic proof; it is not a statutory sign-off.
