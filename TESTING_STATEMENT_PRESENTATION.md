# B20 statement presentation and current-tax working papers

Use disposable books, Statement review. Select fiscal period and branch, review presentation and notes. Division I/AS, Division II/Ind AS or another explicitly reviewed presentation are selectable; NBFC Division III is unsupported. Legal applicability is a reviewer decision. Map every ledger once, review maturity and nature, link note references, retain each disclosure edit before saving. Missing disclosures remain Pending, not automatically 'not applicable'. Reopen and edit a policy: history must retain previous versions. New ledgers require re-review.

Check current/comparative presented P&L and BS, note/classification trace, disclosure evidence, equity roll-forward, ratios and branch matrix. Branch statements retain their clearing; entity clearing proof eliminates equal gross debit/credit only when net is zero. An unmatched residual stays visible and requires review. Test prior-year closing: current profit excludes closing/openings, accumulated results are not counted twice. Equity movements include actual close transfers. Other Schedule III ratios, ageing and transaction-specific Ind AS disclosures must be entered with actual evidence in the review notes, not guessed from aggregate ledger totals. A prepared pack is not statutory certification.

Create an actual current-tax liability ledger in Chart of accounts and map it to Current liabilities: current tax. Map the current-tax expense. Enter effective reviewed base/surcharge/cess, additions/deductions/loss utilisation and evidence. The working calculates PBT excluding tax/OCI, taxable income, component tax and incremental provision after all already-booked current-tax expense. Preview, then explicitly approve. Repeating the same target must not post twice. Change target downwards: reverse only the excess; change books after preview: block approval. Tax law eligibility, MAT/AMT and deferred tax require separate evidenced review and manual journals; this calculation never assumes rates or automatically posts them.

Export statement pack and tax working to both Excel/PDF; verify literal evidence strings, numeric INR cells, repeated headings, full notes, source policy ID, period and mixed scope. Exports and policy edits must not create vouchers. Save/reload the demo vault. Source standard reference: MCA amendment https://www.mca.gov.in/Ministry/pdf/ScheduleIIIAmendmentNotification_24032021.pdf ; primary Schedule III text https://wm.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/schedule-iii . Accountant verifies current applicability, evidence and all required disclosures before client sign-off.
