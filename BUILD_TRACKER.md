Baseline: version 0.3.1, reviewed 29 October 2026 against the uploaded master BRD and subsequent user clarifications. Clarifications take precedence over conflicting BRD text. This is a delivery plan, not a claim of statutory compliance. No new application functionality was implemented by creating this tracker.

Statuses: COMPLETE = the stated narrow deliverable exists; PARTIAL = some functionality exists but the completion criteria below remain; NOT BUILT = no implementation found; DEFERRED = explicitly excluded from this phase. Windows/client acceptance remains a separate gate even for existing features.

## Agreed scope

- One legal entity with JP Nagar, Kammanahalli and Vaikom branches; combined reports are entity-wide aggregation, not multi-company consolidation. Include inter-branch clearing reconciliation and eliminate internal balances where appropriate.
- Single-user Windows desktop application; pastel Indian tricolor theme; no application password; offline accounting AI. No hosted-model processing of client data.
- Financial reporting outputs must be available in Excel (.xlsx) and PDF. This applies to current reports and future financial statements, cash flow, depreciation/tax schedules and MIS/executive packs as they are built (user clarification, 30 October 2026).
- Earlier financial years, not only FY 2026–27. All tax rates, thresholds and depreciation schedules must be editable and effective-dated. Validate legal applicability rather than treating illustrative BRD rates as authoritative.
- All 15 hospital collection modes remain supported, including mixed services and split payments. Keep the current earned-treatment interpretation of Trtmt Pymt RECD; explicit advances remain liabilities.
- External-drive backups; no mandatory cloud storage. Mailbox connectivity is separately authorized internet access, not online AI.
- JP Nagar and Kammanahalli: eight beds each initially. Vaikom under construction; operating date and capacity must be configurable rather than inferred from relative dates. No fixed ₹4.50 crore project budget.
- No customer/vendor ageing, OCR, statutory audit-trail certification, multi-company engine, or mandatory passkey in this phase. Keep basic change history and integrity checks.

## Customer bookkeeping delivery requirement

The application supports a bookkeeper preparing records for a customer. Creating a ledger master in the COA is distinct from producing the account's transaction book. Version 0.3.1 has account masters and a flat general-ledger listing, but does not provide the complete customer-ready ledger books required here.

The first release must deliver a selected period's individual ledgers or all selected ledgers together, covering cash, each bank, the 15 collection categories, expenses, taxes, assets, liabilities and capital as applicable. Each export identifies the customer/legal entity, branch or combined entity scope, reporting period, ledger code/name, currency, opening balance, voucher-backed movements, running balance and closing balance. Include pagination and preparation date for printed/PDF records. Ledger views and exports must agree with the trial balance and source vouchers, including prior-year openings, reversals, transfers and empty-period accounts with brought-forward balances. Exporting records must not change the books. This requirement does not add AR/AP ageing or invoice scheduling.

### Shared patient account

Use a shared Patient A/C (patient billing/collections control account) where a billing-to-collection workflow needs one; do not require or automatically create a ledger for each patient. Track its movements by branch and reporting period. Patient names/IDs are optional source references, not separate ledger masters; invoice and receipt references may be retained for traceability. No patient-wise ageing or patient subledger is required.

For upfront receipts, allow direct posting from cash/bank/payment clearing to the appropriate service revenue accounts without forcing a redundant Patient A/C entry. Where billing and collection are recorded separately, debit the shared Patient A/C against the appropriate revenue accounts, then credit it against the actual collection accounts so revenue is recorded once. Keep the 15 revenue categories distinct; the shared Patient A/C does not replace them. Keep unearned patient/package advances in a separate liability account until earned, and handle refunds/reversals explicitly. Customer delivery in this tracker means delivering books to the hospital client, not generating an account for each hospital patient. Include this workflow in B03/B05/B10/B19 acceptance checks.
