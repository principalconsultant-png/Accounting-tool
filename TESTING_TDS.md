# B09 TDS accounting and challan reconciliation — 0.15.0-dev

This is the functional B09 bookkeeping implementation for reviewed configured resident-payee sections, including 194J, 194C and 194I. It provides dated source/rate/party snapshots, entity-wide threshold assessment, deduction/catch-up journals, existing-source registration, challan payments, partial allocation, CIN revisions, reversal controls, outstanding balances, section summaries and Excel/PDF exports. Target-Windows/client acceptance and verification of actual statutory inputs remain separate gates. It does not file returns or independently validate legal applicability, certificates or government CIN data.

## Setup and migration

Vault format/schema 10 adds TDS records/allocations/details/control mappings. Formats 1–9 remain readable. Existing saved-state integrity is checked before migration; the first upgraded save requires the external original-format backup and byte verification. Keep that backup because older executables cannot read format 10.

In Tax setup create the applicable effective-dated TDS rule, including rate, section, category, single/annual thresholds and basis. Blank means unknown, not zero. Configure Exceeds/At least, whole eligible base/excess over annual threshold/current-transaction-only, threshold aggregation group and paise/whole-rupee rounding. Distinct legal natures can use separate groups under one section. Prior versions remain immutable. Default references are not automatically verified current law.

Create/version the payee with category and PAN when known. The same party ID and matching known PAN identities aggregate across all operating units. Different threshold groups, sections and financial years remain separate. Do not reuse a party ID for a different person. Automatic assessment requires known PAN/category; reviewed manual mode handles verified exceptions without guessing a missing-PAN/certificate rate. TAN and other entity details may stay blank for bookkeeping but must be completed/reviewed before statutory use.

## Deduction workflows

- **New expense + TDS:** enter posting date and actual earlier-of-credit/payment tax point, source reference, gross, legally reviewed deduction base, expense/PPE/inventory/CWIP ledger and payment/payable control. Gross may include GST while base excludes it only when appropriate. The entry debits gross cost, credits net supplier amount and TDS payable. For separately claimable GST, first prepare the correct GST invoice and then use the existing-payable workflow instead of debiting gross to one cost account.
- **TDS on existing payable:** select the original active payable-credit voucher and actual gross/base. It records only debit payable/credit TDS, so expense/GST is not posted twice. A zero-tax source still records its base for cumulative assessment without creating a journal. Linked gross cannot reuse the same source capacity, including through backdating. Review the actual supplier balance; shared controls do not constitute a vendor ageing system.
- **Register prior TDS:** for tax already posted in a generic/imported journal or opening credit, enter the actual existing TDS-credit voucher, amount and control ledger. This adds tax metadata without another deduction journal. Use the actual source/recognition date for historical reporting and the actual tax-point date/FY. It supports carry-in/earlier records without inventing another cost or tax credit. Source credit cannot be reused. Records with no new journal can be voided with a date/reason; financial vouchers are reversed separately.
- **Catch-up deduction:** debit a genuine supplier payable and credit TDS without adding another expense/base. Automatic assessment shows target less previously deducted tax; reviewed manual adjustments require the verified source/reason. If deduction against a small new invoice cannot absorb catch-up, record the verified source target/actual amount and use a separate payable catch-up rather than forcing a negative supplier payment.

Automatic annual crossings can catch up prior eligible bases using their stored rate versions. Current-only and excess-base methods behave differently and must be selected intentionally. Manual entries retain the reviewed tax-liability target (blank defaults to actual manually deducted tax). If actual deduction is zero but verified liability exists, enter that target explicitly; a later automatic catch-up can recover it. Do not use a zero manual target to defer a known liability. Manual adjustment targets represent additional reviewed liability; enter zero target if the adjustment merely collects an already recorded target. Automatic actions do not silently overturn manual exceptions.

The preview identifies aggregate bases, historical deductions, target, catch-up and exact lines. Resident/section/base review and explicit approval are required; changing fields/books invalidates the preview. Rates are snapshots, not retroactively rewritten when masters change. Duplicate party/section/source/FY references are blocked. Late recognition can retain a prior tax-point financial year while posting in an open accounting period. Existing posting locks and account/branch controls apply.

## Challans and allocation

1. **Record challan payment:** enter actual bank/payment date/reference and positive tax rows `section,branch,year,tax`. Year is the liability FY start, not necessarily the payment year. One payment may span units/sections and future allocation can be partial. Every branch balances independently. Interest/late fee use a separate expense account/unit; they cannot settle deduction tax. No bank instruction is sent by this app.
2. CIN may initially be unknown. **CIN details** records append-only BSR/serial/deposit-date/reference revisions. BSR requires seven digits, serial five; preserve leading zeros. A reviewed CIN needs complete details and cannot currently identify two payments. This is bookkeeper verification, not an online government check.
3. **Allocate challan tax:** choose deduction and payment tax row, matching section/unit/liability FY, positive amount and a date on/after both. A payment or deduction can be settled partially across multiple allocations, but never beyond its remaining tax. An advance challan may be allocated when the deduction exists. Allocating creates no extra journal/payment count.
4. **Undo allocation** uses a date/reason and preserves prior-period allocations. Undo allocations before reversing linked payment/deduction vouchers. Reverse/void an existing-payable tax treatment before reversing the original payable source. Histories, source snapshots and allocation evidence remain traceable.

## Reporting and exports

Use the posting-period From/Through controls for annual or quarterly review and the global branch selector for unit/entity scope. Reports include signed period deductions/reversals, PAN/rule rate/tax FY/tax point, all outstanding deductions as at the end date, active challan tax credits including earlier periods, CIN and separate charges, section summaries and ledger opening/movement/closing/unclassified proofs. Posted-date periods and tax-point financial years are explicitly distinct.

Excel and PDF exports contain all five report sections plus contents; JSON includes the source/audit-support records. Allocated payments remain separate from liability recognition. Manual/unregistered TDS ledger movements are exposed rather than silently attributed to a payee. Unknown identifiers/manual methods remain visible. Change history voucher tracing links source deductions, payments and allocation records. Report/export operations do not modify books.

Over-deduction corrections use reviewed dated reversals/re-entry and allocation undo; unallocated challan credits remain visible for later review. Statutory refund/return amendment, nonresident/salary regimes, certificate validation, due-date/interest calculations, TRACES/FVU/return submission and certificate generation are not claimed by B09's bookkeeping scope. Interest/fees can be recorded from actual reviewed amounts. This does not add AR/AP ageing.
